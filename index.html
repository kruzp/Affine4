Questions people ask

Why not just build it myself?

You can, and plenty of people start. The hard part is everything after: writing the words, making it look right on phones, getting found on Google, and keeping it working. We do all of it. Your part is answering a few questions.

What if I don't like the preview?

Then you don't pay anything. Tell us what's off and we'll change it, or walk away. We never ask for a card to show you a preview.

What does the 12 months mean?

After launch, your plan runs for 12 months. After that it goes month to month, and you can cancel anytime.

Do I own my website?

Your business name, words, photos and web address are yours from day one. If you leave after your first 12 months, we hand your site over to you.

What do I need to give you?

Answers to our questions, your logo if you have one, and a few photos of your work. No photos yet? We'll help you with that.

How fast are changes?

Text or email what you want changed. Most changes are done within two business days.
